How this works

What you're looking at

The kitchen at 409 Tara Road, traced from the vector geometry of Studio 4 sheet A08 in the approved consent set — not measured off a picture. The consented drawing sits underneath, pinned to the same millimetre grid, so anything you draw here lands where it would on site.

Three things to try

1 · Move something. Click a cabinet and drag. It snaps to walls and to its neighbours. R rotates, arrows nudge 10 mm, Alt while dragging ignores the snap.

2 · Drag the scullery wall. The partition is draggable. Selecting it dimensions both sides at once, so you can see exactly what a deeper scullery costs the kitchen run.

3 · Start clean. Switch the preset to Kitchen — redesign for the same shell with the cabinetry cleared and the original ghosted underneath.

The numbers that decide it

Every unit reports the clear distance from its working face to whatever is straight ahead. Under 900 mm is flagged as tight; 1,200 mm and over reads as comfortable. The consented galley is exactly 1,200 between bench face and island, so a deeper island eats straight into it.

Worth knowing

The scullery is 671 mm deep — a 630 bench behind full-height cavity sliders that run to the floor, not a walk-in. Because those doors reach the ground, nothing can sit in their track, and the app will tell you if something does.
